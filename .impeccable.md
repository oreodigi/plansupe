## Design Context

### Users
First-time founders and small-business operators use PlanSupe on their phones while turning an idea into a practical opening plan. They need to understand what applies to their category, choose only what they need, and keep costs and timing visible without needing project-management expertise.

### Brand Personality
Clear, grounded, and encouraging. PlanSupe should reduce uncertainty without making business setup feel simplistic or playful.

### Aesthetic Direction
A calm, mobile-first launch workspace built around pale sky-blue surfaces, deep navy text, and purposeful orange highlights. Rounded cards, clear module pictograms, short labels, and generous spacing should make the product feel approachable without becoming childish or looking like a generic admin template.

### Design Principles
- Reveal complexity progressively: basics, modules, requirements, then review.
- Never select or create requirements without the user explicitly choosing them.
- Keep budget, timing, and selection counts visible at decision points.
- Make every primary action comfortable for one-handed mobile use.
- Use plain business language, descriptive icons, and visible system feedback.
- Keep selected modules as the primary navigation and place secondary tools in a consistent top or bottom navigation.
- Use orange for primary action and attention cues; use sky blue for structure, navigation, and progress.
